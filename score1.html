The Scoring System (beta version)


To help you calculate your Healfi-Score and to identify your health areas subject to improvement, you need to fill out the Health Measurement Questionnaire below.

The maximum possible score at this stage of the Healfi Game is 1000 points.

The Healfi Scoring System is the most accurate health measurement tool on the Internet today and we are very thankful to all the people who fill out this beta-version so that we can make improvements for even more accuracy.

If you have any questions, feedback, ideas or just need to talk to somebody, please drop a message in our Suggestion Box. Thank You! (please use this link before or after you have completed the questionnaire to avoid starting from scratch again).
