Feng Shui



Feng Shui is the ancient Chinese art of enhancing and harmonizing the flow of energy in your surrounding for better health, wealth and personal relationships. Feng Shui has evolved over thousands of years into a very detailed science in itself and Healfi will only mention a few points and recommend further in-depth literature.


Example

Who do you think is the most healthy person?
The one who has the shelves on the right or on the left?

In this case the person with the shelves on the right is most probably in better health than the one on the left.

A Feng Shui assessment gives a rough idea of a person's health, but it is not enough as all parts of a person's life need to harmonize for a perfect health.

As you can see, the Feng Shui part of a person's life is fairly easy to assess.
