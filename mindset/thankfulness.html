Thank everybody you meet and deal with for their contribution to making your life better (the waiter, the postman, your spouse, your employees, your boss, ...); the huge positive impact of thankfulness on our health has now been scientifically proven. As an example: 70% of people quit their jobs because they do not feel valued (=their bosses forget to thank them for their contribution to the company).

In the wonderful book The Hidden Messages in Water you can read about how thankfulness impacts water (and we are made of around 70% water...).

Do also not forget to thank the people you have been invited to. There is always a lot of effort behind the preparation of dinners, parties, etc.

What a wonderful world!
