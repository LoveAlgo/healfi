Healfi's aim is to:

  1. Help you understand the dynamics of change and why it is difficult.
  2. Give you tips and tricks to make it easier and quicker for you to change to better habits.

1. Understanding Change

Studies have shown that you need 3 to 4 weeks of constantly applying a new habit because the new habit (or anything you learn) needs to pass the critical factor to get through to your subconscious mind. And this only works by repetition (or by shock, but that can be really painful and dangerous).

Children don't have this critical factor, which is why they learn so much faster.

A classical scenario of change looks like this: you start your new habit, everything goes fine for a while, then you hit the BRICK WALL. Here you have two options:

  1. Not giving up.
  2. Giving up.

Most people give up which is understandable, because the feeling you get is that your goal seems totally impossible to reach unless you have supernatural willpower.
People who are giving up are then turning to some kind of distracting factors (read addictions) like drugs, overworking, drinking, making a lot of money, sex, shopping, overeating, etc. With obvious health consequences.

Now, once you are aware of this brick wall, you can just laugh about it, because you knew it would come in your way. Just the fact of laughing at it, will make the brick wall loose its power and it will be easier to break through the wall. This is why optimistic people with humor who do not take life too seriously, seem to struggle less in life.
