Also called auto-suggestion, affirmations is simply repeating to yourself for a few weeks something you want to achieve, like a new habit. The idea is to "reprogram" your subconscious in a new positive way. Compare this method with the rehearsals needed for a great golf swing or a good stage performance.

Simple positive affirmations can do more for a person's health than most other healing techniques used today.

Healfi is appealing to the world's scientific community to start more detailed research and experiments to better understand the power of affirmations.
