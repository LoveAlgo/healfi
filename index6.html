Water is life

A lake is the landscape's most beautiful and expressive feature. It is earth's eye; looking into which the beholder measures the depth of his own nature.
~ Henry David Thoreau.

Treat water with respect and gratitude. In other words, do not waste water unnecessarily. Obviously, some countries are more fortunate with the availability and price of water than others, but that does not mean you have to waste it.

In the wonderful book The Hidden Messages in Water you can learn more about why you should respect water.
