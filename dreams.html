Dreams of an Amazing Future

The aim of the lines that will follow is to stimulate the creativity of individuals and corporations to do their best to help the world reach a state of advanced civilization as fast as possible. Thanks to the Internet, this future might be closer than what you think.

Three things will be required in order to reach this Amazing Future:

  1. Each individual needs to take responsibility for his health and his life.
  2. Most social institutions and controls need to be rethought or eliminated completely.
  3. A handful of specific breakthrough technologies.

1. Individuals

Every human being knows right from wrong.

2. Society

An advanced civilization noticed that so much evil in the world came from a single source: money. So they put in a lot of effort to build a society that did not need the thing that most corrupts people and can be detrimental to their health.

The other source of corruption is obviously control: so they built a society without any controls: no border, no governments as we understand them today. This is now possible because people understand the huge rewards they receive when they do "the right thing".

3. Technology

An advanced civilization has mastered the following technology breakthroughs:

  • Indefinite source of energy
  • Indefinite source of water
  • Teleportation
