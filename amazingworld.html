What could an amazing world look like?

Below are a few points that Healfi thinks could describe an amazing world:

1. Health

  • No diseases
  • People feel really good
  • People are radiant and have a lot of energy
  • People have beautiful bodies
  • People understand all subtleties of how health really works
  • People take responsibility for their health
  • People are honest with themselves as to what they need to feel good

2. Relationships

  • Trust
  • No divorces
  • No loneliness
  • Community caring for you
  • Tolerance
  • Helping mindset
  • No shyness
  • People do not try and control each other
  • Positive use of networking
  • Abundance of sex and love

3. Transport

  • No pollution
  • No noise
  • No waiting
  • No accidents
  • No stress
  • Teleportation?

4. Water

  • Clean
  • Energizing
  • Abundance
  • Free water

5. Energy

  • Clean
  • Abundance
  • Free energy

6. Education

  • Individualized
  • Focusing on children's passions and interests
  • New topics like health, creativity, emotional handling, learning to use intuition, lucid dreaming, spirituality, self-development, etc. need to be taught
  • Helping children to find their life goal(s)

7. Work

  • Should be in line with your life goal
  • Should have a meaning
  • Should be fun
  • No negative stress
  • No pressure to work for money
  • Kind and supporting colleagues
  • High level of trust and freedom

8. Environment

  • Nature everywhere
  • No noise pollution
  • No air pollution
  • No water pollution
  • No visual pollution
  • Many animals around

9. Living

  • Sustainable living
  • Community villages where everybody knows each other
  • No concrete jungles
  • Unique housing for unique individuals/families
  • Slow living
  • Quiet neighbourhoods
  • No motor vehicles
  • Plenty of exercise possibilities
  • Good entertainment
  • Wellness centres
  • Feng Shui homes
  • Sharing (WiFi, swimming pools, cars, etc.)

10. Society

  • No controls (borders, laws, ...)
  • No laws, only some necessary rules
  • Governing law = common sense
  • No money?
  • Exchange of products and services?
  • Cooperation
