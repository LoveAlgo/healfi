The Rewards

In the game of health, you can reap 3 different kinds of Rewards:

  1. Individual Rewards
  2. Collective Rewards
  3. Advanced Special Rewards

1. Individual Rewards

Below you will find a list of some potential rewards that can be reached on an individual level:

- a wonderful relationship with your life partner - less sickness
- an amazing and fulfilling sex life - less worrires
- a healfi bank account - less stress
- more vacation - less addictions
- better work-life balance - less enemies
- a well-trained body - less fears
- better sleep - less debts
- better and more friendships - less useless expenses
- more fun in life - less problems
- more social assertiveness - less anger
- more satisfying work/occupations - less resentments
- more variety in life - less boredom
- more pleasures - less jealousy
- more knowledge - no rat race
- more meaning to life - less mistakes
- more creativity - less complaining
- long life - clutterfree home

2. Collective Rewards

Some rewards can only be reached by teamwork when enough people play specific cards correctly:

- cleaner water to drink - cleaner air to breathe
- fresher food to eat - more beauty to look at
- more beautiful Nature - less visual pollution
- less noise pollution - less information pollution

3. Advanced Special Rewards

The Advanced Special Rewards will only be given to people who manage to reach advanced levels of health. The extent of these Special Rewards goes beyond what most people can imagine, which is why we will not describe them here for the time being.
