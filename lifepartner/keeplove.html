Life Partner

Given the statistics, this is a tricky one where most people do not have an answer. Life is somehow built up in a way that you get punished if you rest on your laurels. This is as valid for relationships as for your personal growth.

You can almost see life as a video game where you continuously have tests to pass to get to the next level. Healfi believes that the best way to pass these tests is to be aware of them, just laugh at them and never give up!

This is easier said than done when you stand in front of the brick wall. But remember: the wall loses some of its power when you are perfectly aware that it will come in your way.

When the wall appears, do your best to raise your energy level, it will be much easier for you to deal with it.
