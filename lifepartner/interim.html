Healfi wants to promote "interim relationships" for single people who have not found their soul mates yet. Interim relationships can but do not need to involve physical contact, and should aim at having the partners help each other to grow, learn more about the other sex and get more self-confident for the day when Mr./Mrs. Right will appear in their lives. And not feel lonely.

So try and find somebody that you really like and respect, but that you know is not a person you will marry. Most probably, you already know some potential interim mates. Example: if you want a blond soul mate, find a dark-haired person for an interim relationship until you have found the "one".
