Probably the main reason why people have difficulties to find their respective soul mates, is that they do not know EXACTLY who they are looking for.

Start to write down all the physical attributes and personality traits you want in your soul mate.

Now, do not settle for less!

So, if you for example look for a non-smoking partner, you are losing your time if you go around and flirt with smokers. Be consistent with yourself.

Now it is also extremely important that you do whatever you can to meet up to the standards you are looking for. So if you want a top-model partner, common sense tells us that a couch potato lifestyle will not attract her/him to you.

You need to have absolute faith that you will find the right partner one day. And when you find your soul mate, you will not need any pick-up tricks or verbal convincing. It will feel right straight away.
