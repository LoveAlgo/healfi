The Ringing Cedars Books

In Healfi's relentless search for books and other material that can make a real difference in people's lives, we have finally found something that should blow your mind!

It is a series of 9 books from Russia, The Ringing Cedars. Even with their deep, life-changing content, they read like a novel. So what kind of books are they? The English translator first described them as "Star Trek meets the Bible" to give people an idea of the contents. But it is much more than that...

... it is a plan... that finally gives us a real hope for a wonderful future on Earth! And this plan is no utopia, it has already started, mostly in Russia, but even in some other countries as well.
