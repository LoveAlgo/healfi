Laws of Nature for Health

Healfi has so far identified 12 laws of Nature for health. When people have health issues, they have most probably ignored at least one of these laws.

Healfi's aim is to help people UNDERSTAND these laws in order to stay healfi.

You can compare these laws to the Law of Gravity. The only difference is that gravity has immediate consequences, which is why most people respect gravity.

The most important Laws of Nature for Health that Healfi has so far identified are:

1. Have a Goal in Life 7. Be Social
2. The Golden Rule 8. Have Fun!
3. Positive Thinking 9. Always Continue Learning
4. Be Thankful 10. Respect the Environment
5. Help Others 11. Listen to Your Body
6. Be Honest With Yourself 12. Take Responsibility
