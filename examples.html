Examples of the Healfi Approach

The Healfi approach goes beyond thinking only about physical exercise and nutrition when it comes to health. Let a few examples help you understand:


Healfi Juggling

Prescribed by far too few doctors, juggling can be a very positive tool for your overall well-being. When you learn to juggle, the Healfi approach sees at least the following benefits for your health:

  • You do some mild training.
  • You learn something new that you can improve all your life.
  • You are able to entertain other people.
  • You train your vision.
  • You improve your eye-body coordination.
  • You have fun and cannot take life too seriously when you juggle.
  • It forces you to concentrate on the balls so that you forget your daily worries! And if you have daily worries, tune into this website more often in the future, Healfi is working on creative and entertaining solutions to help you feel much better!

So, how do I learn to juggle? Start here... Good luck!


The Healfi Walk

Millions of people living in cities around the world could walk or bicycle to their workplace instead of driving or using public transport.

The overall benefits go far beyond the mere physical exercise necessary to walk from A to B. The Healfi approach sees the following positive impacts on your health:

  • You respect the environment.
  • Ever heard anybody being stressed walking to work? Sitting behind the steering wheel or in a packed subway it is another story
  • You get some fresh air.
  • You do not congest traffic/subway and respect other people who really need motorisation.
  • You will emit no noise pollution. Thank you!
  • You will be able to cope better with stress as you have an overall better physical fitness.
  • You will get more daylight.
  • And last but not least, you will be more awake when you come to work, and thus do less mistakes and need less coffee

The Healfi Barbeque (starring Magnus):

The other morning, my roommate Magnus told me happily: I have lost a pound (ca. 450g) even though I organized a barbeque yesterday

How is this possible? He had chips, beer and red meat with the barbeque

So I went through the whole event to see what he did right.

  • A few days ago Magnus told himself and a few friends: I want to organize a barbeque! So far so good, but where the Healfi approach comes in is that he actually DID it and kept his promise to himself (and others). Always doing what you say you are going to do is worth many training passes in the fitness studio.
  • On the day of the barbeque, he left the office earlier than usual and scored a few points on the work-life balance
  • Then he unleashed stuck energy (Feng Shui) by taking off the dust from the grill and taking it out because it hadnt been used for at least two years.
  • After that, Magnus trained his brain and solved a technical problem with the gas pipe to the grill.
  • Then he had to carry the gas tank back and forth to fill it up at a gas station and thus got some decent physical exercise: the grill is on the roof terrace (7th floor without an elevator).
  • Then, he also made an effort to choose and buy various food items for the barbeque.
  • He also got many Healfi points just by organizing an event to bring some joy in other peoples lives.
  • The guests arrived  positive social interactions are extremely important for a well-rounded health.
  • Magnus really enjoyed the food, ate slowly and was able to relax and scored far more points than if he had, as usually, eaten in front of the TV.
  • The positive Healfi snowball-effect continued after the guests left: he had now a boost of energy to clean up everything in the kitchen.

Now he can be really proud of himself

But the Healfi story does not end yet: now, instead of watching TV, he read a book before going to sleep and slept much better as usual because he wasnt bombarded with all those electromagnetic waves before he went to sleep.

SoNo wonder that he woke up earlier than normal, singing and happy!
