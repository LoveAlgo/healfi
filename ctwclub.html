Change The World Club

Dear all,

Thank you so much for your interest in the Change The World Club (CTW Club)!

Below I have put down some thoughts about my current visions for the Club.

This is just a first draft, so please give me as much feedback as possible! Not even the name of the Club is definitive yet, so if you have other ideas, please let me know.

For a start, I will use my homepage healfi.me as a virtual information and meeting point. Bookmark this link as I will not have a direct link from the welcome page.
Then the people who are in the same location can meet physically. It now looks like we have people on the US West and East Coasts, and in Europe. So we will probably have meetings in LA, NYC and London for a start.

Let's build an amazing world!

Henrik


Mission

To discuss and describe what an amazing world could look like and then search for creative big-scale solutions. Then we should present our findings to people and institutions who can make things happen.

Draft agenda for the Club

1. Describe the headlines of what an amazing world could look like so that we have a fair idea of what we are aiming for. We need as much consensus as possible here. That link is a first draft of my own vision of this amazing world.
2. "Be the change you want to see in others": make sure that we do whatever we can and all help each other to improve ourselves first (=walk the talk).
3. Club statutes?
4. Any member fees?
5. Member selection criteria?
6. Website?
7. Communication?
8. List of members?

Questions to all of you:

  • A) As we are in so many locations, what do you think is the best way to get to know each other? A forum on the website? A Facebook group? Local meetings? E-mail/mailing list? Share CVs?
  • B) Do you know any other visionary persons who might be interested in joining/supporting the Club?
  • C) What can you personally contribute with? Knowledge? Experience? Speciality? Network? Money? Time?
  • D) What would you like to improve yourself to "be the change you want to see in others"?
  • E) What topics are you personally most interested in? (health, environment, energy, ... see the amazing world description).
  • F) Any specific role you would like to have within the Club? For my part, I see myself as coordinator and "creative connector" and my dream goal is to find a solution for teleportation.

Thank you so much for all your feedback (general and for the specific points), the best might be that you send it to my e-mail directly: contact@healfi.me.
