Measure Your Health Questionnaire


The following questionnaire is the only one on the Internet to calculate roughly how healthy you are. Just by answering the questions, you will get an idea of your life areas subject to improvement.

The maximum Healfi-Score is 1000 points.

Please avoid the "BACK"-button until you have finished the whole questionnaire. When you have finished, you will get your score and a summary of the questions and your answers. We recommend you to copy and paste this summary into a text file and keep it.
