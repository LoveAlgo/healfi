BMI-Calculator

Your Body Mass Index is equal to your weight in kilograms divided by your height in meters squared. It is expressed as BMI=kg/m2. A healthy BMI should lie between 19 and 25, but be aware that it is only a very rough health measure and many other factors need to be taken into account as well.
