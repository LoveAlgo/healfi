The Healfi Approach

Healfi® has so far identified 12 areas that influence on people's health.


The aim is to balance all these areas, so for example you cannot compensate a total lack of social life with running marathons, nor compensate your love life with more work.

But don't worry, you do not need to do everything "correct" to feel healthy! It is more important to find a healfi balance between all areas.
