What are your dreams?

The main reason why people are frustrated in life is that they are not following their life purpose and not doing what they are passionate about in life. The clearer you are about what you really want in life, the better you will feel and the easier it will be to live this dream life.

Below are some questions that should help you find out what your passions are:

  • If you had 100 million dollars on your bank account and did not "need" to work, what would you be doing?
  • As a child, what did you dream about becoming when you grew old?
  • If you would start your career again, what would you do? What would you study?
  • "When my life is ideal, I am...": take the The Passion Test and write down what your life would look like when it is "ideal"
  • Being perfectly true to yourself, would you still do the job you are doing now? Or have the relationships you have now? Or live where you are living now?


Good. Now that you better understand health and know what you really want in life, you need to work on getting there. So how do you reach your dreams?
