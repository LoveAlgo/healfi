To understand your parents' love, you have to raise children yourself.
~ Chinese Proverb.

Help Your Parents

Respect your parents and help them as much as you can and show them gratitude for having brought you up. Help them with household tasks and technical issues (computers, electronics, etc.).

Help Your Children

As a parent, you have a tremendous influence on what your children become in life. You are far more important then all their teachers combined. Make sure that they get healthy habits from the start!

Help Your Grandparents

It does not need to be much, a phone call now and then. Try and visit them regularly, there is so much that they can teach you!
